1.Kompanie/IR23, Re-enacted

Since 1993, IR23 has been an anchor of the Central Powers line at the Great War Association site in Newville, Pennsylvania. We recreate authentic, yet enjoyable experiences of everyday life and battle in 1918, from our frontline trenches to our 35-man, underground bunker on-site.

Newville, Pennsylvania

Feldzug 1918: Your First Event

The 1918 tactical re-enactment at Newville, PA is held twice per year. These events are the highest-fidelity First World War re-enactments in the world.

With IR23, you'll experience…

Frontline Trench Warfare

The GWA site allows us to stage mock WWI battles on a scale that feels real in the moment. We simulate combat with blank fire rifles and machine guns, air-powered mortars, real biplanes, and, of course, hundreds of re-enactors on both sides.

You will get a taste of WWI combat: clearing enemy trenches, patrolling No Man's Land in the pitch darkness, ducking for cover under artillery, shoring up positions with sandbags, and taking part in large-scale assaults with other regiments.

Bunker Life

IR23 possesses the largest authentically-built, underground bunker on the Central Powers line — a true “time capsule” with room for up to 35 men. Inside our bunker, you will live, eat, and sleep just as the German soldier of 1918 did.

Our bunker is also the home base of our unit, where we hold our Kneipe (sitting around the table singing German songs, food, drink, and games), unit meetings, and generally, a Saturday night post-combat gathering open to any GWA unit.

Unit Values

Do you belong in IR23?

Good fits for our unit culture are seeking a healthy balance of kit & culture authenticity, living history experiences, and fun.

Authenticity

Our kit standards are above average. All our members want to uphold this reputation and work between every event to improve their impression, even with small details.

Fraternity

We're a group of guys you'd want to spend a weekend with. Members are voted in only if they're a fit for the unit. We maintain collective buy-in and real camaraderie without forcing it.

Gemütlichkeit

In English, “enjoyment”. Although we wear military uniforms, we're really a social club. Our members are active year-round online, and we make a point to enjoy our time together at events.

MANAGING COSTS

WE'LL HELP YOU AFFORD IT

IR23 supports beginning members practically, because building a quality Imperial German infantry impression can be expensive and time-consuming.

  • 01

    Buy once, buy right. Our golden rule for recruits is, ask before you buy! We help you source correct kit items right off the bat, so you don't have to replace inferior gear later.

  • 02

    Loaner gear. We do not expect Rekruten to have their full kit for their first couple events. As you build a kit, members will happily supply loaner items to fill out what you have yet to acquire.

  • 03

    Access to deals. Buy used gear from unit members, at events, or online. We will connect you with the marketplace for affordable kit.
